## Why this round exists (diagnosis from Round 1)

Round 1 (`dial_stocks_2026-11-14.md`, FAIL both years): the signals were not pure noise —
they beat the random-entry median in 5 of 6 series-years — but the frame churned ~6,700
trades/year (~27/day) at first-signal-per-stock-per-day, and the +1.0%/−0.5% geometry lost
even on the best random draws. **Untested hypothesis: frequency is the poison.** Round 2
changes ONLY the selection frequency. Signals and exits are Round 1's, verbatim.

## R2 selection rule (the ONLY change)

Per component, per calendar date, across all 25 names: take **AT MOST ONE** trade — the
most extreme candidate of the day:

- **DIAL-R:** excursion past the trigger — LONG candidate: 0.05 − %B; SHORT candidate:
  %B − 0.95. Largest excursion wins (this is min %B among longs / max %B among shorts, and
  it compares across sides: if both a long and a short extreme exist the same day, the
  larger excursion takes the single trade).
- **DIAL-T:** largest directional move % at the signal bar (side × (close−open)/open).
- **Ties (either component):** earliest signal-bar stamp, then alphabetical symbol.

Entry and exit of the selected candidate are exactly as computed in the pool (next bar
open; frozen geometry). No re-entry, no second trade per component-day. The combined
DIAL-Stocks R2 book is the union of the selected T and R trades (≤ 2/day).

## GATE (frozen, per year, both years; bar applies to the COMBINED book)

- **Placebo (frequency-matched):** per series per year, 200 draws, fixed seed 20261115.
  Each draw preserves the real book's exact trading dates and per-date trade count; every
  placebo trade = uniform random name among the names tradable that date × uniform random
  entry bar (stamps 09:20–15:10 present that stock-day) × uniform random side, exits under
  the identical geometry on that stock-day's real bars. Percentile = % of draws whose
  total is below the real total. **Must beat the 95th percentile in EACH year.**
- Halves (H1 = Jan–May, H2 = Jun–Oct) both positive; month table Jan–Oct; ex-top-3
  (total minus the 3 largest trade nets) positive.
- **PASS BAR (per year, both must pass):** placebo met AND halves positive AND ex-top-3
  positive AND monthly pace (total/10) ≥ ₹7,500 in 2026 and ≥ ₹5,000 in 2025 AND no more
  than 4 red months (month net < 0) in the year. WR reported plainly (no WR bar).
  Components are scored against the same legs and reported alongside; the binding verdict
  is the combined book's.
