## 5. THE HONEST GATE — what approval would and would not do

Approving this list does **not** put these names live. It starts a backtest:
the enlarged 37-name universe is scored with the frozen book rules (EKAM
intraday Cand-1/Cand-2, Stock Book, EKAM-Daily) against the frozen-25
controls, and the additions join the live universe **only if the enlarged
run does not materially degrade any book** — because this morning's wide-100
test proved more names can make selection worse (EKAM wide ex-APARINDS lost
to the 25 in 5 of 6 windows). Sector coverage is the owner's goal; book
quality is the gate. The performance test of exactly this proposal follows
in `sector_additions_test_2026-11-17.md`, under the verdict bar frozen in
the addendum below — frozen **before** any scoring.

# SPEC ADDENDUM — verdict bar for the additions test (FROZEN 2026-11-17, before scoring)

- **Universe under test:** frozen 25 + the 12 proposed additions = 37 names.
- **Books:** EKAM intraday CAND-T5 · EKAM intraday S2 · Stock Book
  (build_book machinery, within-machinery comparison) · EKAM-Daily (EKAM-2c,
  official-close basis, ₹1 cr / 60-session liquidity screen, both sides).
- **Basis:** equity cash, per 100 shares PROVISIONAL, ₹40/trade, paper only.
  Judged per year (2025, 2026 Jan–Oct), never pooled; November slice separate.
- **Controls:** EKAM 25-name published totals (T5: 2025 +₹62,164.57 ·
  2026 +₹63,883.60 · Nov +₹24,528.65; S2: 2025 +₹45,300.64 · 2026 +₹72,605.30 ·
  Nov +₹20,662.60) · Stock Book 25-name within-machinery run · EKAM-Daily
  official-close 25 control (2025 +₹63,621.50 · 2026 +₹1,50,777.40).
- **Per book, per year, the enlarged run is:**
  - **IMPROVES** — net above the control, and max drawdown not worse than
    1.5× the control's drawdown;
  - **HOLDS** — net within 10% below the control (or above), and max
    drawdown not worse than 1.5× the control's;
  - **DEGRADES** — net worse than the control by more than 10%, **OR** max
    drawdown worse than 1.5× the control's.
- **FIT TO JOIN LIVE** requires **no book DEGRADES in either year**. Any
  DEGRADES verdict = the additions are not fit as a set; the report then
  names which books degraded and what the added names themselves contributed.
- **Added-names contribution** is reported separately for every book and
  year: how many of the 12 added names actually traded, and their net
  subtotal inside the enlarged run. (Selection interacts across the
  universe — caps bind globally — so this subtotal is descriptive, and the
  headline verdict stays the whole-book comparison above.)
- Nothing live changes whatever the outcome. Live addition waits for the
  owner's word on the test report.
