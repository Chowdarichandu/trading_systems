## ⚠️ Constituent-bias limitation (read before any number above)

Constituent lists are the **current (Nov 2026)** published lists applied backwards to 2025–2026. Names removed from an index during 2025–26 — usually for poor performance — are absent from the whole test; names added later are present from the start. This survivorship/look-ahead bias **flatters** the results. The random-selection placebo shares the same lists and the same bias, so the SAPTA-vs-placebo comparison is fair even though the absolute nets are flattered. SAPTA failed *despite* the flattering bias.

**Sources:** all seven lists are the official NSE Indices (niftyindices.com) IndexConstituent CSVs, fetched 2026-11-17, saved in `sapta_constituents/`. No index substitution was needed — all 7 primary indices (Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma, Nifty Auto, Nifty Metal, Nifty FMCG, Nifty Realty) fielded a full pick list at every quarterly ranking.

## How it works (frozen design)

- **Selection:** re-ranked on the first trading day of each quarter. Rank = trailing 126-trading-day return, computed only from closes before the ranking day. Constraints at ranking: price ≤ ₹2,000 (owner rule), median 60-day traded value ≥ ₹5 crore (close×volume), full 126-day history.
- **Strategy:** daily momentum breakout, LONG only. Close above the prior 20-day highest close → buy next open. Close below the prior 10-day lowest close → sell next open. Max 10 positions at once; a name dropped at a re-rank exits at that open.

## What the measurements show (why momentum broke here)

- **2025 expectancy was negative, not just unlucky.** Win rate ~38–39% with an average winner (₹7.4–7.9k) barely larger than the average loser (₹5.7–6.3k) — at that hit rate the book bleeds steadily. Whipsaws (loss within 5 days) were only 11–15 trades per size-year; the damage was broad, not a few bad ticks: 8 of 12 months negative for SAPTA-14 in 2025.
- **2026 worked for the pool, not for the ranking.** Win rate rose to ~43–44% and the winner/loser ratio improved (≈2.1×) — but random names from the same pools did as well or better. Picking the *top* trailing performers added no measurable edge over picking *any* eligible name in the same indices.
- **Concentration by name.** SAPTA-21's entire window net (+₹1,17,248) is less than its single best name: LAURUSLABS +₹94,535, plus SAILIFE +₹51,470. Breadth: 24 of 45 traded names positive (SAPTA-14), 26 of 52 (SAPTA-21). Worst names: WOCKPHARMA −₹48,720 / −₹49,010 (picked repeatedly on trailing strength, kept breaking down), UNOMINDA −₹46,760 (SAPTA-14), HCLTECH −₹33,910 / −₹31,850.
- **November fragment (observation only — NOT a promotion).** The Oct-2026 selections rode November well: trades *entered* in Nov 2026 netted +₹32,292 (SAPTA-14, 6 trades) / +₹31,095 (SAPTA-21, 4 trades), and the Nov exit-month rows read +₹86,987 / +₹1,21,751.
