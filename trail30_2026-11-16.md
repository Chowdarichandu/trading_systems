# TRAIL30 TEST — 2026-11-16

Owner's question (20:08 IST): *"With 30 points and trail how much
accuracy."* Spec frozen before scoring: `trail30_SPEC.md`. Paper
research only; nothing running was touched. Lots: Bank 30, Nifty 65;
cost ₹220/leg.

**The variant:** each leg keeps its own frozen exit until the
premium first touches **+30** (completed-bar high, registered at
that bar's close — the books' own arm convention). From the next
bar the fixed target is REMOVED and the only exits are a **trailing
stop at (running peak − D)** — level set from the peak through the
prior bar, breach on the bar's low, fill at the level or at the
open on a gap-through — and the leg's own square-off.
D ∈ {10, 15, 20} points, pre-registered, no other dials.

**Controls reproduced EXACTLY before any variant was scored:**
Bank TUNED STACK v2 **+₹127,351.50 / 111** (leg-for-leg vs the
frozen eve19 file) · Bank legacy v21 **+₹83,650.50 / 99** (replay ==
recorded on every leg) · Nifty TUNED v1 **+₹103,050.25 / 74**
(standing-geometry replay, 0 mismatches). Mirror audit: the trail
walk with the trail disabled and level fills reproduces every
recorded control exit on all **284 legs**. Timing audit: **0
problems** on all 9 variants. Independent re-sum of the trades
file: exact for every stream × variant × period.

## The answer to the question — accuracy first

| Stream | Control WR | TRAIL30 WR (any D) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank stack | 59.5% | **73.9%** | +14.4 pts |
| Bank legacy | 52.5% | **69.7%** | +17.2 pts |
| Nifty TUNED v1 | 51.4% | **64.9%** | +13.5 pts |

**Yes — the trail raises accuracy on all three books.** The win
rate is the same at D = 10, 15 and 20 within each stream, because
the winner set doesn't depend on D: the trail activates on 86 of
111 stack legs (74/99 legacy, 47/74 Nifty) and every activated leg
exits through the trail — and a trail exit is a win by
construction, since the level never sits below entry+10. The only
exceptions are gap-through fills: 4 stack legs (5 legacy) where the
exit bar **opened** below the level and below entry, booking small
losses (−₹272 to −₹679); on Nifty all 47 trail exits are wins.

Same law as every other early-grab form measured on these entries:
the books are paid by the runner tail (median max run +52 to +93
points). The trail steps off at the first D-point dip after +30 —
and the dip after the first push is the normal path of a winning
trade. On the Bank books the TIGHTEST trail (D10) keeps the most
money; on Nifty the widest (D20) does. Neither pattern rescues the
total.

**Where the trail genuinely helps:** worst drawdown on both Bank
books — stack −₹7,221 → **−₹5,249** (D10), legacy −₹13,240 →
**−₹6,614** (D10). On Nifty it makes drawdown WORSE (−₹13,670 →
−₹23,605 at D10): the non-activated legs' losses pile up in
Feb–May while the winners that used to offset them are truncated.

The trail is the most accurate of the forms that let the trade
keep running — and it still keeps only a fifth of the money. Every
form that touches the exit after +30 pays for its accuracy out of
the same runner tail. The exits are not the wound; November's is
the entries dying in the first 15 minutes, which is the side D1
(the adopted Bank lead) works on.
