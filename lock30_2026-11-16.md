# LOCK30 test — keep a first target at +30 points? (2026-11-16)

Owner follow-up (18:59 IST) to today's small-target test: "How why
don't we keep the first target of 30 points?" Two forms of that idea
were still untested — tested here. **Both FAIL on all three streams.
No PASS anywhere, so no forward shadow is built.**

- **LOCK30** (the executable 1-lot form): ride the original exit
  (−50 stop / +90 / runner) until the premium first touches +30;
  from the next bar the stop becomes entry+5 — a locked small
  profit. No further ratchets.
- **HALF30** (arithmetic reference — **requires 2 lots to execute
  literally; on this 1-lot record it is a points blend**, one ₹220
  cost per leg): at +30, half exits at +30; the other half rides
  the original exit untouched.

This is not the Nov-3 ladder (that had a −20 hard stop and ratchet
locks at +5/+15/+25 — it failed mainly on the tight stop) and not
S30 from this morning (that sold EVERYTHING at +30). LOCK30 keeps
the −50 stop and the full runner; HALF30 keeps half the runner.

Paper research only. Nothing running was changed. Real money ₹0.

- LOCK30 changed 55 of 111 exits; 51 legs exited at the +5 lock.
  Of those 51, **31 were legs the control rode to +₹98,193.50 of
  wins**; only 20 were losers it saved (−₹30,437). On Bank the
  locked exit itself is still a loss after cost: +5 points × 30 =
  ₹150 < ₹220 cost → net −₹70. That is why the win rate COLLAPSES
  to 31.5% — most "locked profits" book as small losses.
- HALF30 touched +30 on 87 of 111 legs. Trade-off, plain numbers,
  no PASS: win rate better (62.2% vs 59.5%), worst drawdown better
  (−₹4,595 vs −₹7,221), both halves positive — but the total is
  43% lower and it puts a red month (Aug −₹304) into a book that
  had none. Fails the frozen rule on total and red months.

## November counterfactual (Nov 2–16, shown, never selecting)

| Stream | Control | LOCK30 | HALF30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank stack (4 legs) | −₹2,680 | −₹5,230 | −₹3,580 |
| Bank legacy (7 legs) | −₹3,640 | −₹3,131.50 | −₹3,040 |
| Nifty v1 (1 leg) | +₹3,036.50 | +₹105 | +₹2,383.25 |

One leg shows the whole mechanism: the stack's Nov 4 trade — the
month's only Bank winner — touched +30 early, dipped back through
entry+5 at 10:05 (LOCK30 books it at −₹70), then recovered and the
control exited at the runner stop for +₹2,480. Premiums that touch
+30 very often dip 25+ points before the real move; the +5 floor
sits exactly in that dip.

## Why — the same law, third time today

1. **A +30 touch is common; a straight run to +90 is not.** 87 of
   111 stack legs touched +30, but 51 of them fell back through
   +5 before resolving. The dip after the first push is the normal
   path, not the exception.
2. **The runner tail is the profit.** Every way of taking money
   off at +30 — sell all (S30 this morning: +₹18,333), lock at +5
   (LOCK30: +₹52,305), sell half (HALF30: +₹72,842) — keeps only
   14% / 41% / 57% of the stack's +₹127,351.50. The ranking is
   exactly the ranking of how much runner each form leaves alive.
3. **On Bank, +5 points doesn't even clear the cost** (₹150 vs
   ₹220), so a "locked profit" books as −₹70 and the win rate
   falls instead of rising. On Nifty it clears cost (+₹105), the
   win rate rises — and the total still halves.

## Verdict

**FAIL — all six stream × variant cells.** Frozen rule (total >
control AND zero red months AND placebo beaten): no cell passes;
LOCK30 on Nifty fails all three legs of the rule. No forward
shadow is built for either variant, and no cron line is reported
(the 21:20 IST slot stays empty).

The +30 first-target question is now measured in all three forms
— sell-all, lock, and half — on all three streams, plus the Nov-3
ladder before them. Every form pays for its smoother ride with
40–85% of the book's money. The books keep their original exits.
