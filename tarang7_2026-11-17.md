## 4. Diagnosis — the seed for Design 8

**What failed is ENTRY TIMING.** Design 7's one-bar histogram tick
confirms nothing: after a beyond-band close, price on 5-minute
F&O-name bars continues in the same direction more often than it
snaps — 63% of resolved trades stop out before targeting, while
the trades that merely drift (square-offs) are net positive. The
entries fire mid-move, one bar after the extreme, before any
actual turn exists. It is not the name mix (losses are broad
across all 127 names, no concentration rescue, the same names do
not repeat as worst across years beyond MAZDOCK/POLICYBZR) and it
is not primarily the exit geometry (flipped entries lose both
years too; random entries lose as well — the geometry is a
constant drag, the entry stream decides the sign, and this entry
stream is early).

**Design 8 seed:** (i) delay the confirmation — enter only when
price closes back INSIDE the band after a beyond-band excursion
(the band re-cross), with the histogram still rising, so the turn
is demonstrated rather than ticked; (ii) carry the volume finding
inverted — on reversion entries the quiet snaps revert and the
violent ones continue, so Design 8 measures low-volume preference
(the inverse of T7-V's veto) as an allocation-side candidate.
Exit geometry, universe and caps stay frozen until the entry side
shows a timing edge worth optimising exits against.
