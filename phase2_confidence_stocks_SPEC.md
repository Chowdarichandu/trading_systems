## Factor audit (uniformity rule, frozen)
A factor enters the score ONLY if it is (a) knowable at the leg's entry bar
and (b) available for 100% of legs in EACH year (a partial join biases the
score — the same ruling that dropped room ratio / VIX in the Bank run).
Coverage is measured in-run and printed; any factor failing (b) is dropped
and recorded, never partially applied.

### Factors IN (subject to the uniformity check)
- **S1 Entry time** (carried: `entry_time`). Raw value = minutes after
  midnight. Score = 1 − mid-rank empirical CDF within the year pool
  (earlier entry scores higher; mechanical rationale, as Bank F3: more room
  before the 15:20 square-off). Knowable at entry: YES.
- **S2 Trend alignment** (causally joined day state). Regime label per date
  from REGIME-CLASSIFIER-1, replicated exactly from the book's own machinery
  (`regime_maps_from` in stocks_engine_2025_validation.py + `regime_labels`
  in stocks_sarvaritu_logger.py): prev_close = prior trading day's NIFTY
  close; ma50 = mean of the 50 closes strictly before the date; slope =
  ma50(date) − ma50(5 axis-days earlier); UP iff prev_close > ma50 and
  slope ≥ 0; DOWN iff prev_close ≤ ma50 and slope ≤ 0; else FLAT; first 5
  axis dates unlabelled. NIFTY closes: `volrix/nifty_daily_2019_2026.csv`
  (full history → ma50 exists for every leg date). VALIDATION (pre-scoring):
  labels must reproduce the November paper log's own regime values on the
  dates the series covers (2026-11-02 DOWN, 2026-11-03 FLAT) and agree with
  labels computed from the logger's own store
  (`data/progressive/NIFTY/spot_daily.csv`) on Jan–Oct 2026 dates at ≥99%.
  Score: LONG on UP or SHORT on DOWN → 1.0 · FLAT day → 0.5 · against → 0.0.
  Knowable at entry: YES (state as of prior close).
- **S3 Trend strength** (same join). Raw = |prev_close/ma50 − 1|. Score =
  mid-rank empirical CDF within the year pool. Knowable at entry: YES.
- **S4 Prior-day relative volume** (causally joined:
  `stocks_daily_volume.csv`, all 25 names, 2024-11-18 → 2026-10-30). Raw =
  prior trading day's volume / mean volume of the 20 trading days ending on
  the prior day. Score = mid-rank empirical CDF within the year pool.
  Knowable at entry: YES (prior day complete before the open).
- **S5 Same-stock confluence** (carried: date, stock, entry_time). Raw =
  count of book legs in the same stock on the same date with entry_time ≤
  this leg's entry_time (including itself; simultaneous same-stock entries
  count each other — they are generated by the same pre-entry decision
  point). Score = mid-rank empirical CDF within the year pool. Knowable at
  entry: YES (uses only legs already entered).

### Factors audited and DROPPED (recorded, not silently omitted)
- Entry price / exit price / exit_reason / setup / INV round: carried only
  by `stock_engine_causal3_hist_*` for F4 / INV-LONG / INV-ABOVE — joins
  952 of 2,427 Jan–Oct legs (39%). Partial → DROPPED.
- Prior-day stock move %: NOT carried in the gated store; per-stock daily
  bars on disk (`data/progressive/<SYM>/spot_daily.csv`) start 2025-01-01
  with no Dec-2024 tail and uneven end dates, so the reaction-day move is
  not uniformly reconstructible for the full two-year population. DROPPED.
- Component family / stock identity as score inputs: categorical identity
  is not a confidence measure; using a family's or stock's own realised
  Jan–Oct P&L to rank its trades would be circular (in-window outcome
  fitting). NOT used as score inputs (reported descriptively only).
- Side (LONG/SHORT) standalone: no non-arbitrary numeric confidence
  transform; direction enters only through S2 alignment. DROPPED as a
  standalone factor.

## Composite score and grid (pre-registered)
Composite = equal-weight mean of S1..S5 (each in [0,1]). Computed per leg
within its own year. Grid per year: rank legs by composite descending; keep
top 70% / 60% / 50%; ties sitting on the cut score are all kept; actual kept
counts and cut values reported. (If any S-factor fails the uniformity check
in-run, the composite is the equal-weight mean of the surviving factors and
the drop is stated in every output.)

## Scoring per kept subset (per year)
Kept trades, total, WR, ₹/trading-day (denominator = the full book's
distinct trade-days in that year's Jan–Oct window, fixed for all subsets),
red months (Jan–Oct month totals), halves (Jan–May / Jun–Oct), ex-top-3
(3 largest legs of the subset removed).

## Placebo (pre-registered)
200 random subsets of the SAME kept size drawn from the year's legs
(seed 42). Statistic = subset total. PASS requires the kept subset's total
to exceed ≥95% of the placebo draws, in EACH year.

## Pass bar (pre-registered, NOT to be moved)
A grid level PASSES only if in EACH year ALL hold: WR strictly above the
full-book WR for that year · total ≥ 60% of the full-book year total ·
no new red month (kept red-month set ⊆ full-book red-month set) · both
halves positive · ex-top-3 positive · placebo condition met. Any leg
failing in either year = FAIL for that grid level, with the failing leg
named.
