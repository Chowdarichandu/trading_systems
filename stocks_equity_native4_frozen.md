# Stocks EQUITY — Exploration Round 4 — FROZEN SPEC (written BEFORE scoring)

**Status: FROZEN 2026-10-30, before any round-4 scoring run. NOT adopted,
NOT a live system. Paper only, no orders.**

**EQUITY CASH intraday only. LONG/SHORT language only — no options, no
premiums anywhere. Real money: ₹0.**
**Every rupee figure: provisional per 100 shares, ₹40/trade flat cost.**

## Why this round exists

Rounds 1–3 transplanted index-system entries (V5, Skill, (lA), Volrix)
onto the 25 stocks and failed on entries, exits (0.5–1.25% targets,
9-combo grid) and every selection filter, in BOTH halves of Jan–Oct
2026. The entry signal source is the failure. This round tests
STOCK-NATIVE signals — signals generated by the stock's own day —
consistent with the user's philosophy that stock moves are
event/news-driven. No V5/Skill/(lA)/Volrix signal is used anywhere
in this round.

## Universe, window, data

- 25 screenshot stocks (same list as prior rounds).
- Window: Jan–Oct 2026, trading days from the NIFTY daily calendar
  in `data/progressive/` (Jan–Oct = 203 days; Jan–May = 99 days;
  Jun–Oct = 104 days; October = 1–28 Oct, 18 days; 29 Oct missing
  from cache, stated, not a zero day).
- Data: each stock's `spot_5m.csv` (bars timestamped by start time)
  and `spot_daily.csv` (previous-day close/high/low).
- Event source: `stock_event_calendar_jan_oct_2026.csv`.

## Portfolio and accounting rules (frozen)

- **Uncapped portfolio**: every qualifying stock trades; max 1 trade
  per stock per day per family. The user's minimum-2-valuable/day is
  a floor, not a cap — trades/day is reported, never capped.
- Quantity 100 shares. Net per trade = sign × (exit − entry) × 100
  − ₹40, sign = +1 LONG, −1 SHORT.
- All trades intraday; nothing carries overnight.

## Exit rules (frozen, identical for every family)

- **Primary exit T10**: target +1.0% from entry, stop −0.5% from
  entry (mirrored for SHORT).
- **Secondary exit T125**: target +1.25% from entry, stop −0.5%.
- Entry is at a bar's close (per family definitions below); the exit
  scan starts on the NEXT bar after the entry bar.
- On any bar, **stop is checked before target** (stop-first on a
  same-candle tie). Exit price = the stop/target level touched.
- If neither level is touched, exit at the **close of the 15:10 bar**
  (square-off). Bars after 15:10 are ignored. If the 15:10 bar is
  missing, exit at the last available bar's close.

## Halves and survivor rule (frozen)

- Half 1 = Jan–May 2026 (date ≤ 2026-05-31); Half 2 = Jun–Oct 2026
  (date ≥ 2026-06-01). October slice = date ≥ 2026-10-01.
- **Survivor rule: a family is a survivor only if its net
  (provisional per 100 shares) at the primary exit T10 is positive
  in BOTH halves.** T125 is reported alongside but does not create
  survivors. Near-misses (positive in one half only) are reported
  as observations only, never as systems.
- Every family reports: trades, win %, target-hit %, net,
  expectancy, trades/day — for Jan–Oct, each half, and October.

## Families (frozen definitions)

### N1 — Gap continuation
- Gap % = (open of the 09:15 bar ÷ previous daily close − 1) × 100.
- **N1a**: gap ≥ +1.0% → candidate LONG; gap ≤ −1.0% → candidate
  SHORT. Confirmation: close of the bar timestamped 09:20 must still
  be beyond the day open in the gap direction (LONG: close > open;
  SHORT: close < open). Entry = close of the 09:20 bar.
- **N1b**: identical, with the gap threshold at |gap| ≥ 2.0%.
- If the 09:20 bar is missing, no trade.

### N2 — Gap fade
- Same gap days as N1 (|gap| ≥ 1.0% for **N2a**, ≥ 2.0% for **N2b**),
  same entry point (close of the 09:20 bar), but direction is
  OPPOSITE the gap (fade toward the previous close), with NO
  confirmation condition — N2 trades every qualifying gap day.
- N1 vs N2 is an empirical question; neither is favoured in advance.

### N3 — Stock opening-range breakout (ORB)
- Opening range = bars timestamped 09:15, 09:20, 09:25 (the bars
  spanning 09:15–09:30). ORH = max high, ORL = min low of those bars.
- Conditions (both, known before any breakout):
  - OR width % = (ORH − ORL) ÷ day open × 100 ≤ 1.5%.
  - Prior-10-trading-day ADR ≥ 2.0%, ADR computed exactly as in
    round 3: mean of (daily high − daily low) ÷ daily close × 100
    over the prior 10 daily bars (minimum 5 required). The ADR ≥ 2%
    "room to travel" condition is motivated by round 3's F1 pocket
    (high-range stock-days were the least-bad pocket), stated here
    in advance, not fitted in this round.
- Signal: the FIRST bar timestamped ≥ 09:30 and ≤ 15:05 whose close
  is beyond the range — close > ORH → LONG at that close;
  close < ORL → SHORT at that close. One trade per stock-day max.

### N4 — Results-reaction momentum
- Reaction day: from the event calendar, rows with
  event_type = `results`. Effective reaction date = `reaction_date`
  when present and within [event_date, event_date + 7 days],
  otherwise `event_date` (the convention used in prior rounds).
  The reaction date must be a trading day in the stock's own daily
  data and inside the window.
- Move by 09:30: move % = (close of the bar timestamped 09:25 ÷
  previous daily close − 1) × 100. If |move| ≥ 1.0%, direction =
  sign of the move (trade WITH the reaction, not against it).
- Entry = close of the bar timestamped 09:30 (known at 09:35;
  recorded entry_time 09:35). Exit scan from the 09:35 bar.
- **N4b**: the identical rule on reaction days of single-news events
  (event_type in {management_change, large_order, fda_approval,
  block_deal, sector_policy, pricing, corporate_action}; dividend
  rows excluded as mechanical). Reported separately; if the sample
  is under 10 trades it is labelled insufficient-sample and cannot
  be a survivor.

### EXCLUDED — N5 first-hour range extreme fade

Its exclusion is part of this frozen spec.

## Union rule (frozen)

If two or more families survive (both-halves positive at T10), they
are combined into one union: all survivor trades pooled, dedup on
(stock + date + direction) keeping the earliest entry, scored at
T10, reported for Jan–Oct, both halves, and October. If fewer than
two families survive, no union is scored and that fact is stated.

## What this round does NOT do

- No re-use of V5 / Skill / (lA) / Volrix entry signals.
- No filter, threshold, or stock list chosen from round-4 results.
- No claim from a single half, a single month, or October alone.
- A survivor is a stage result only: November 2026 paper is its
  validation, not this Jan–Oct in-sample window.
