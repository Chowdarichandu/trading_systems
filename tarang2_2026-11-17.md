## Family-level diagnosis — what Rounds 1+2 have now measured

1. **The VWAP/EMA trend state has no entry-timing edge on this universe and
   window — measured three times.** Round 1's trend∩pullback combination
   failed the placebo in both years with a flip placebo 2–3× its own net;
   Round 2's trend-only book (B) again fails the placebo in both years, sits
   below the random *median* in 2025, and is again beaten by its own flipped
   entries in both years. Trend-state establishment entries systematically
   precede adverse intraday moves on these 25 names. Any future use of this
   trend state as an entry trigger would be re-testing a thrice-measured
   negative.
2. **The RSI pullback timing is real but sub-bar in 2025.** TARANG-A is the
   only rule in the family that beats its flip placebo in both years and the
   random median in both years, and it clears p90 in 2026 by a wide margin —
   but in 2025 it reaches only between the median and p90. The pullback
   timing edge exists; at the frozen parameters it is not strong enough in
   the weaker year to meet the family's own gate. Per the Round 2 discipline,
   no parameter was tuned and no Round 3 is designed in this run.
3. **Where the money actually sits:** (a) the +0.8%/−0.5% exit geometry on
   these 25 liquid names pays almost any intraday participation — the random
   placebo *median* year is worth ~₹2.0–2.1 lakh per year at the same trade
   counts; (b) on top of that, the short side — fading RSI rallies (A) —
   contributes ~73% of the best candidate's net. The family's measured edge,
   such as it is, lives in the geometry plus the short-side RSI fade, not in
   trend timing.

**No Round 3 was designed or run, and no parameter was tuned** — per the
Round 2 tasking and `tarang2_SPEC.md` Section 6.
