## Status — SHADOW, and why this exists

This is a **shadow** log. The November primary for stocks is
N4-V3 (event-reaction), in its own spec and log
(`stocks_n4_november_paper_spec.md` /
`stocks_n4_november_paper_log.csv`). A second shadow, P2-INV1,
runs in its own spec and log
(`stocks_p2inv_november_shadow_spec.md`). P2F runs alongside
both; it changes nothing about either.

P2F is the **UNGATED** variant of the P2 post-reaction
follow-through rule discovered in the per-stock reaction-profile
build (`stock_reaction_profiles.md`, engine
`stocks_reaction_profiles.py`, frozen and scored 2026-10-31).
The gated P2 variant (a leave-one-out per-stock continuation
gate) produced only 3 in-sample trades and was untestable; it is
**NOT** carried. P2F is the ungated rule, exactly as scored.

> **Hypothesis (generated by Jan–Oct 2026 data — NOT established
> by it):** when a stock's results reaction day moves at least
> 2% (either direction), the move tends to continue into the
> next trading day strongly enough that entering at the next
> open, in the reaction direction, with a small target, is
> net-positive after costs.

## The rule — exact (engine-faithful)

Definitions below are the scored definitions of
`stocks_reaction_profiles.py` (the engine is the authority; the
profile report's prose agrees with it on every point).

1. **Universe:** the 25 watchlist stocks.
2. **Event population — RESULTS EVENTS ONLY.** In-sample, the
   population was the `event_type = results` rows of
   `stock_event_calendar_jan_oct_2026.csv` — results
   announcements only. Single-stock news, management changes,
   orders, FDA items and every other event type were **not** in
   the scored population and do **not** qualify in November.
3. **Reaction day R:** the calendar `reaction_date` when
   announcement date ≤ reaction_date ≤ announcement + 7 days,
   otherwise the announcement date itself. Working convention:
   an announcement made after market hours (or on a
   Saturday/holiday) has its reaction day on the **next trading
   day**; an announcement known before/during market hours
   reacts the same trading day. R must be a trading day in the
   stock's own daily data.
4. **Reaction-day return:** `close(R) / close(previous trading
   day) − 1`, in %, **rounded to 2 decimal places** (previous
   close = the stock's own previous trading day's daily close).
   The threshold test uses the rounded value.
5. **Candidate:** a results event whose **|reaction-day return|
   ≥ 2.00%** (exactly ±2.00% qualifies) AND whose R+1 — the
   stock's next trading day after R — exists. Trade date = R+1.
6. **Direction:** the sign of the reaction-day return — positive
   reaction → **LONG**, negative reaction → **SHORT**. (The ≥2%
   gate means the return is never zero.)
7. **Entry:** the **open of the first 5-minute bar (the 09:15
   bar) of R+1**. No intraday trigger, no confirmation, no
   re-entry.
8. **All qualifying events are taken.** One trade per qualifying
   event; no per-stock cap, no per-day cap, no direction filter.
   Multiple stocks qualifying on the same R+1 are all traded.
9. **Exits** — the common exit engine of the profile build
   (identical to the round-5 N4 engine): the exit scan starts
   on the bar **after** the entry bar; within a bar the **stop
   is checked before the target**; if neither level is touched,
   exit at the close of the **15:10** bar (if no 15:10 bar
   exists, at the close of the last bar at or before 15:10).
   - **Primary: T125** — target **+1.25%** / stop **−0.5%**
     from entry (target price = entry × (1 + sign × 1.25%);
     stop price = entry × (1 − sign × 0.5%), mirrored for
     SHORT).
   - **Shadow column: T10** — the same entries re-scored at
     target **+1.0%** / stop **−0.5%**, recorded per trade in
     the log's shadow field. The shadow column informs; it
     never changes the verdict exit.
10. **Net per trade** = sign × (exit price − entry) × 100
    shares − **₹40** flat cost.
11. **Corporate-action artefacts — NOT adjusted in-sample, and
    not adjusted in November.** The scored engine applied no
    exclusion or correction: VEDL's 2026-04-30 "reaction" of
    −64.77% was the demerger price adjustment, not a results
    reaction, yet it qualified as a candidate and its trade
    (2026-05-04 SHORT, net −₹178.50) sits **inside** the
    48-trade / +₹12,835.88 discovery figures. Excluding it would
    have raised the in-sample T125 net to +₹13,014.38; the
    discovery does not depend on it either way. November rule,
    frozen: the same — if a reaction-day move is identified as
    a corporate-action price adjustment (split / bonus /
    demerger ex-date), the trade is still taken per the rule
    and flagged `CORPORATE_ACTION_ARTEFACT` in the log's notes.
    No silent exclusions.

## November event source

- **Primary source:** the November watchlist
  (`stock_event_watchlist_nov2026.md`) **plus a same-day check
  of the stock's exchange filings (BSE/NSE announcements)** to
  classify each day's actual announcements. **Only an actual
  results announcement creates an event.** A filing that is not
  a results announcement (dividend record date alone, trading
  window notice, investor call, other news) does not qualify.
- Watchlist results dates known at freeze time (several are
  carried/provisional in the watchlist — the filing check, not
  this list, is what creates an event): NTPC reaction Mon
  2026-11-02 (Sat 31 Oct board); ITC and NATIONALUM
  2026-11-02; NHPC 2026-11-04; GLENMARK and PFC 2026-11-05;
  HINDALCO 2026-11-06; TATASTEEL reaction Mon 2026-11-09;
  KALYANKJIL 2026-11-10; OIL 2026-11-12; BDL reaction Mon
  2026-11-16; IRCTC 2026-11-17 / 2026-11-18. Nine watchlist
  stocks had no November date confirmed at freeze time
  (HDFCBANK, TVSMOTOR, UNITDSPR, OBEROIRLTY, INDUSTOWER,
  WIPRO, HINDCOPPER, ASTRAL, HINDUNILVR) — the daily filing
  check is what catches their results if announced.
- **Boundary rule (pre-registered):** an event belongs to the
  November sample by its reaction day R. A reaction on the last
  October trading day (Fri 2026-10-30 — e.g. GODREJCP, board
  2026-10-29) whose R+1 is Mon 2026-11-02 produces a November
  trade and counts in the November sample. Symmetrically, a
  reaction on the last November trading day whose R+1 is the
  first December trading day is taken and counted in the
  November sample.
- **Unclassifiable days:** if a day's filing cannot be
  classified as results / not-results, or R+1 price data cannot
  be obtained, log one row with stock = `NONE`, direction =
  `unclassifiable`, and the reason in the notes. Never guess an
  event into existence; never silently skip one either.

## Judging rule — pre-registered, judged once at month-end

Judged once, after the last November trading day (including any
boundary trade per the rule above), on the **primary exit
T125**: November net (provisional per 100 shares), win %, and
trade count, together.

- **November net positive at T125 with ≥ 8 trades:** P2F is
  upgraded to a **candidate alongside N4-V3** — it earns a
  further frozen decision (e.g. a second fresh window). It is
  NOT adopted on one month.
- **November net negative at T125 (with ≥ 8 trades):** P2F is
  **killed** — recorded as a Jan–Oct in-sample artefact, no
  re-tuning, no threshold change, no direction split after the
  fact.
- **Fewer than 8 qualifying events in November:** the test is
  **inconclusive** — neither passed nor failed — and the shadow
  **extends to December** under these same frozen rules, judged
  at December month-end on the combined Nov+Dec sample against
  the same ≥ 8-trade / positive-net bar.
- **November net exactly ₹0 with ≥ 8 trades:** not positive,
  so no upgrade; not negative, so no kill — treated as
  inconclusive and extended to December as above.

**No parameter changes during November:** no change to the 2%
threshold, the results-only population, the R+1 open entry, the
exits, the cost, or the judging rule. Observations may be noted
in the log's notes field; they change nothing until the
month-end judgement.
