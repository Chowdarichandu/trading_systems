## 2. Diagnosis — the two-sided line closes

**What Design 28 measured.** The threshold ladder for
the down-gap side is now priced at the book level at
1.5%, 2.0% and 2.5%, and it is not monotonic: 2.0% was
the best rung (8 of 9, ex-top-3 −₹2,914.55) and 2.5%
is the worst (7 of 9, ex-top-3 −₹15,107.69, month
floor re-broken). The failure mechanism is identified,
not mysterious: in 2026 the deeper down-gaps the cap
selects are continuation moves, gross-negative per
trade at every threshold (−₹10.14 at 1.5% in T25-A's
taken set, −₹23.43 at 2.0%, −₹96.61 at 2.5%), while
the band each rung removes contains taken winners.
A selection rule cannot repair a side whose taken
population loses more the more extreme it gets.

**Closure (pre-registered in tarang28_SPEC.md S.0,
from the Design 27 seed — the condition has
triggered):** A-ASYM-25 fails the same leg A-ASYM
failed (2026 ex-top-3). **The two-sided line is
concentration-bound in 2026 and CLOSES.** No further
threshold rungs, no retest of this construction.
TARANG's gated books are the two T25-C packs —
**frozen T25-C (passed 9 of 9, Design 25) and
T25-C + TRAIL (passed 9 of 9, Design 26 WS3)** — both
prepared, both self-tested, both awaiting the owner's
word. Nothing in this design changes them.
