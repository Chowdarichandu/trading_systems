## The one volume-bearing store — and why it cannot substitute

`data/progressive_vol/<SYM>/spot_5m_vol.csv` does carry volume (`timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume`) for all 25 names — but only **2026-01-01 → 2026-10-29** (15,300 bars per name; VEDL 15,291). It has **no 2025 bars at all** and no November 2026 bars. The frozen window's 2025 leg — net positive in 2025, placebo 90th percentile in 2025, ex-top-3 in 2025 — cannot be scored from it, and the spec forbids substituting data for an insufficient store. `data/wide100/<SYM>/` and `data/progressive/<SYM>/` 5-minute files were also checked: same OHLC-only header, no volume. No other volume-bearing 5-minute store for these names exists in the workspace.

## Why no proxy was used

A prior programme build (`vwap_ema_smalltunes_stocks_build.py`) faced the same gap and used an equal-weighted typical-price proxy, disclosed in its own spec. TARANG's frozen spec explicitly forbids that substitution — VWAP's information content *is* the volume weighting; a typical-price average is a different indicator and would silently change the strategy being tested. Under the controls-gate rule (no number quoted whose controls have not passed; no faked inputs), the honest outcome is a stop, not a proxy run.
