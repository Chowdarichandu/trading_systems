**The rule change being tested (user, 2026-10-30 19:47 IST):**
stocks have NO trade-count restriction. A stock that exits can be
traded again the same day on the next fresh signal — re-entry is
the norm for equity; the one-and-done rule belongs to index
options premium decay and was wrongly applied to stocks. NO
portfolio cap, NO per-stock/day cap; one open position per stock
at a time per system. All prior V5 / Skill / (lA) small-target
verdicts were computed at max 1 trade/stock/day and are re-checked
here. Volrix-stock is excluded, stated plainly: its trigger is a
previous-day close ≤ −2% with entry at the next 09:15 open and a
10-session cooldown — one-shot per trigger day by construction,
its signal cannot recur intraday, so there is nothing to re-enter.
N4 / N4-V3 is not part of this re-run (day-level event trigger);
its November paper spec stays as frozen.

## Verdict first — NO verdict flips. There is no survivor.

Under re-entry, **every system × exit is net negative in BOTH
halves of Jan–Oct, all-days and no-event-only — every net figure
provisional per 100 shares.** The best combination under re-entry,
V5 × T125, nets −₹184,394.90 provisional per 100 shares over
Jan–Oct (halves −₹77,033.23 / −₹107,361.67, both provisional per
100 shares); the worst, Skill × T05, nets −₹965,522.30
provisional per 100 shares. Re-entry does not rescue any of these
entries; on totals it mostly scales the loss, because it
multiplies trades and therefore multiplies the ₹40/trade cost.

One honest nuance, reported because the expectancy comparison
shows it: re-entry trades are slightly *less bad per trade* than
first-signal-only trades for most combinations (V5 × T125
expectancy improves from −₹37.86 to −₹27.83 provisional per 100
shares; Skill × T10 from −₹70.47 to −₹50.67 provisional per 100
shares). Later-in-the-day signals are not worse than the first —
they are just still negative. And V5 × T10 / T125 under re-entry
are *gross*-positive in both halves before costs (see §4) — the
frozen ₹40/trade cost is larger than the gross edge per trade.
Under the frozen basis, the verdict stands: fail.

## 5. What this re-run settles

- The earlier negative verdicts on V5 / Skill / (lA) equity
  entries were NOT an artifact of the 1-per-day construction.
  Given the user's actual rule — unlimited re-entry — the same
  entries produce 1.6×–3.9× more trades, slightly better
  expectancy in most combinations, and larger total losses, all
  provisional per 100 shares.
- The entries remain the problem (Rounds 1–4 conclusion
  unchanged): the signal set does not predict a 0.5–1.25% move
  with enough accuracy to clear a ₹40/trade cost, no matter how
  many times a day it is allowed to fire.
- Frequency is a solved non-issue: 18–98 entries/day depending on
  system, against a minimum-2/day floor.
- Nothing from this re-run goes to November paper. N4-V3's
  November paper (event-reaction, a different entry logic) is
  unaffected by this result and stays as frozen.
