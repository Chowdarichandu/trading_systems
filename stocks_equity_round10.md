Definitions (each is the exact complement of a Round 9 gate):

- **INV1** = P2 trades on days when Nifty's previous close is
  **≤ its 50DMA** (inverted G1)

  **≤ its 20DMA** (inverted G2)

| All three | 235 | — | — |

**Verdict on overlap: substantially ONE bet, not three.** INV2
is 93.6% contained inside INV1 (below-20DMA days are almost
always below-50DMA days for this pool). INV3 is a partially
different cut (per-stock, not index-level) but still shares
~58–74% of its trades with the index variants. The three
"discoveries" of Round 9 were three windows on one correlated
below-trend subset — testing one of them on fresh data tests
the hypothesis; testing all three would triple-count the same
evidence.

# PART B — November SHADOW paper spec (frozen)

**Carried variant: INV1** — P2 trades taken only when Nifty's
previous close is ≤ its 50DMA. Full spec:
`stocks_p2inv_november_shadow_spec.md`; empty log:
`stocks_p2inv_november_shadow_log.csv`. **SHADOW status** — it
runs alongside, never instead of, the November primary (N4-V3,
in its own log). No cron/schedule was created in this round;
the parent sets scheduling from the spec file.

**Why INV1, under the tie-break stated before scoring this
round** (both-halves net positive → largest sample share →
simplest definition):
1. All three variants are both-halves net-positive at the
   primary exit in-sample — the first criterion does not
   separate them.
2. **Largest sample share: INV1** — 75.6% of the P2 pool
   (83.2% of Jan–May, 54.2% of Jun–Oct). A shadow test on the
   largest share gives November the most trades and the least
   selection.
3. **Simplest definition: INV1** — one index-level comparison
   (previous Nifty close vs its 50-day mean), no per-stock
   indicator state to dispute.
INV2 has the higher in-sample net and expectancy and is
recorded in the spec as the alternate — it is NOT carried,
because its total is the most March-concentrated (63.6%) and
picking the highest in-sample net after seeing all three would
be exactly the fitting this exercise is designed to avoid.

**Judging rule (in the spec):** November is a hypothesis test.
Net + win% + trade count at T125 judged together at month-end:
a positive November upgrades INV1 to a *candidate* (earning a
further frozen decision, not adoption); a negative November
kills the inverted hypothesis. A month where Nifty stays above
its 50DMA throughout (zero qualifying trades) is an inconclusive
test, reported as such — not a pass and not a fail.
