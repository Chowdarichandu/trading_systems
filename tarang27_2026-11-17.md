The threshold did what a threshold does — it removed
the 1.5–2.0% down-gap band (138 LONG trades in 2025,
196 in 2026) — and the diff convicts the band of
opposite crimes in the two years. In **2025 the band
was a winner**: removing it cost ₹19,748.84 of LONG
profit (the year's total falls ₹21,442.64 vs T25-A,
to +₹2,83,180.72). In **2026 the band was a loser but
not the whole wound**: removing it recovered only
₹5,216.63 of the LONG side's −₹27,227.78, because the
≥ 2% down-gap longs that remain are themselves
gross-negative in 2026 (−₹23.43/trade — the book's
taken subset of the very population the survey priced
at +₹181.40/trade in the raw stream; the |gap|-ranked
cap spends the day's slots across both sides, and on
2026's clustered down-gap days it takes the most
extreme names, the continuation days of Design 25's
T25-B diagnosis). The SHORT side is untouched by
construction and behaves like it (+₹1,49,008.75 /
+₹51,886.60, a small allocation-interaction gain in
2026 from the slots the removed longs freed).

Net effect of the asymmetry: −₹21,442.64 in 2025,
+₹8,290.63 in 2026, drawdown reduced in both years —
and the gate profile improves from T25-A's 7 of 9 to
**8 of 9**. What remains is pure 2026 concentration:
a +₹29,875.45 year whose top 3 trades are worth
₹32,790.00 — the year's profit is still three trades
deep, just less deep than before.
