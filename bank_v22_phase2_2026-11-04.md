## Method (spec fidelity)

- **(a)** Days with a v22 leg exited `stop` (the full −50 stop, net −₹1,720):
  36 days, exactly one stopped leg each. Trigger = first 5-min **close**
  back through the morning-range extreme on the stopped leg's side, bar at/
  after 12:00 and at/after the stop's exit bar. Morning range = the day's
  09:15–11:59 spot high/low — the stopped legs' own setup ranges are not in
  the leg record, so this is the documented approximation the brief allows.
  Entry = ATM (nearest 100 to the entry bar's spot open) at the **next bar
  open**, monthly expiry (store-derived calendar: 2026-01-27 … 2026-10-27;
  for 2026-10-28/29 the store's single in-force chain, 2026-11-23, is used).
  Day-cap: skipped if the day's realised v22 net (legs exited on/before the
  trigger bar — what is knowable when the entry executes) is ≤ −₹3,000.
  **The cap blocked 0 of 16 triggers** (no stop day had realised ≤ −₹3,000
  by trigger time).
- **(b)** Population = all 205 trading days. Trigger = first 5-min close
  after 12:30 beyond the running day high/low built from strictly earlier
  bars. Same ATM/next-bar-open/G0 mechanics. Combined set = sleeve legs on
  days with **no re-entry taken** (the pre-registered day-level rule): 14
  sleeve legs sit on re-entry days and are excluded. On 13 of those days
  the sleeve trigger printed at/after the re-entry trigger (usually the
  same bar — the reclaim close *is* the range break), so no live-trading
  impossibility; the one exception is 2026-07-07 (sleeve 13:10, re-entry
  14:55). A chronological one-afternoon-trade variant was scored as a
  sensitivity: base+chrono = +₹54,643.50 (₹5,464.35/mo) — same conclusion.
- **G0 exits** (frozen November convention): intrabar on premium bars,
  stop before target, entry bar included, fills at the level (stop →
  −₹1,720, target → +₹2,480), square-off at the 15:10 bar open.
- **NC accounting:** (a) 0 NC. (b) 144 triggers → 136 legs; 8 triggers fell
  too late to enter before square-off (trigger bars 15:05–15:25 on
  2026-01-02, 02-11, 02-13, 06-10, 07-30, 08-03, 08-20, 08-31) and are
  listed as trigger-no-time, not traded. 0 contract NCs; 0 path-end exits
  (every scored leg's premium path runs through 15:05).

## What this means for the gap

Phase 2's frequency lever splits in two. The **re-entry half works**:
stop days are not dead days — 44% of them reclaim the morning extreme,
and trading that reclaim adds +₹611.90/month at +₹382.44/leg without
worsening drawdown. Carried forward, **v22 + EVE-06(a) stands at
₹9,756.95/month, gap ₹5,243.05/month**.

The **sleeve half fails**: unfiltered afternoon break-following fires on
70% of days and loses on both sides of the market. Frequency was never
the constraint by itself — Phase 1's arithmetic (+5.76 legs/month at
₹1,016/leg) only closes the gap if ₹/leg survives, and the sleeve's
₹/leg is −₹269.56. Any future afternoon-coverage variant would need an
entry filter the pre-registered spec does not contain; none was tested
here, and this rejection is of the sleeve **as registered**, not of
afternoon trading in every form.

**In-sample caveat (standing):** EVE-06 was registered from one live day
(2026-11-03) and scored on the same Jan–Oct window as everything else.
The re-entry's +₹6,119.00 is a ceiling with a concentration flag
(ex-top-3 −₹1,321), not proof; its forward evidence would be November
shadow days. Nothing in this phase is adopted or armed.
