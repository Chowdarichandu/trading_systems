## Why this keeps happening — the plain reason

These entries do not make their money from many small wins. They
make it from a minority of trades that run far — past +90, past
+150. A trade that reaches +40 usually keeps going; yesterday's
measurement on the stack: the median best run of a trade is +93
points. Booking at +40 keeps every small win and throws away the
big runs that pay for all the losses. That is why the win rate goes
UP and the money goes DOWN, on every entry stream, in both indices.
The VWAP-cross Bank book shows it hardest: its entries need the
bigger move, so the +40 cap turns a +₹24,508 book into a loss.

November says the same: on the stack, the small target made
November worse (−₹4,180 vs −₹2,680); on premium-tape worse
(−₹2,460 vs −₹960); on regime-reversal it turned November's one
positive control (+₹2,141) negative (−₹859). The only November
lines the small target improved are single-trade lines (VWAP-C,
TUNED v1's one leg) and the VWAP-A Bank book, which lost less
(−₹2,740 vs −₹7,840) while still losing.
