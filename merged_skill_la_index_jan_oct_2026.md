  short paper-only, one trade/instrument/day after dedup. Index nets are
  point-scaled cash-equivalents (100 × index points), NOT portfolio rupees.
- **Source A — Skill:** OR-only signals passing the V4-style filters from
  the capped-v2 runs (bias-aligned, room ≥ 200×scale to the next PD level,
  PD bound: CE close < PDH, PE close < PDL), via
  `progressive_capped_v2.candidates_for_month`, index only. Setup rank 0.
- **Source B — (lA):** exactly as in the October merge — BN-confirmed
  rescan morning + band-filtered re-entry, scan-skip to 11:30, afternoon
  stop-on-first-fail to 15:05, PE only, entry = next bar open, stop =
  signal high, target = entry − 2×risk, ₹40 per leg. Nifty (lA) is the
  original (Bank confirms Nifty); **Bank (lA) is the MIRROR (roles swapped,
  Nifty confirms Bank, scale = Bank prev close / 55000) — a provisional
  adaptation, NOT the original (lA).** One candidate per symbol+date where
  a rescan morning exists; setup rank **1** (provisional labelling);
  prospective target net = morning target net; room ratio from the capped
  engine on the rescan signal close (infinite room below PDL maps to 1.0,
  so room ratio is dead among (lA) candidates, as in October).
- **Dedup:** same symbol+date = one trade; **(lA) wins over Skill.**
- **Cap and filter:** prospective target net ≥ ₹500, rank by setup_rank,
  then bias-strong, then room_ratio, then prospective target net
  (signal-time/prospective only). Cap: **max 3 index/ISO week, max
  1 trade/symbol/week** — with 2 symbols the attainable max is 2/week in
  practice. Results gate NEUTRAL.
- **Cap convention (stated, not a re-tune):** the cap is applied **within
  each calendar month**, exactly as the capped-v2 Jan–Oct precedent
  (`run_v2_month` per month) and as the October merge did within October.
  Jan–Oct totals here are the sum of the ten monthly capped series. A
  pooled Jan–Oct cap would let late-September trades (ISO W40) displace
  1 Oct trades and would break the October reproduction check — it was
  not used.
- **Win rate = net > 0 (green) for ALL series.** For (lA) day trades the
  exit is shown as morning exit + re-entry exit, not a single-leg exit.
  (lA) `exit_reason` values "day_green"/"day_loss" are re-entry-inclusive
  day outcomes, not single-leg targets/stops — see §5.

## 5. The (lA) re-entry leg — the key January–October result

October said nothing about re-entry (0 legs fired). Jan–Oct says a lot,
and it is negative:

- **(lA) candidates: 66. Morning outcomes: 28 targets, 38 stops.**
  Of the 38 morning stops, **30 fired a re-entry leg** and 8 found no
  re-entry candidate (day = morning stop alone, −₹29,245 across those 8).
- **Re-entry legs fired (pre-cap): 30.** Re-entry nets sum to
  **+₹51,300** against morning nets of −₹207,425 on those same days,
  for day nets of **−₹156,125**. Only **11 of 30** re-entry days end
  green. The re-entry recovers roughly a quarter of the morning loss
  on those days; it does not turn the set green.
- **Re-entry legs in the capped (lA) series (and therefore in merged,
  which contains all 47 (lA)-capped trades): 22.** Re-entry nets
  +₹25,225, morning nets −₹155,200, day nets **−₹129,975**; 9/22 end
  green; re-entry exits: 12 target, 10 stop, 0 square-off.
- **The other 25 capped (lA) days — morning targets with no re-entry —
  net +₹153,410 (19 green).** So the (lA)-capped total (+₹23,435) is
  +₹153,410 from morning-target days minus ₹129,975 from re-entry days.
  The re-entry days are where (lA) loses its money, concentrated in
  Bank mirror days: e.g. 6 Feb Bank −₹22,855 (morning −₹15,355, re-entry
  stop −₹7,500), 29 Jun Bank −₹24,935 (morning −₹15,645, re-entry stop
  −₹9,290), 14 May Bank −₹18,995, 14 Jul Bank −₹16,030, 8 Jul Bank
  −₹14,995, 11 Feb Bank −₹12,850.
- Re-entry legs fired by month (pre-cap): Jan 6, Feb 6, Mar 2, Apr 1,
  May 3, Jun 2, Jul 3, Aug 6, Sep 0, Oct 0. September and October are
  the only months with zero re-entry legs — the re-entry rule is
  untested in exactly the two most recent months.
- No re-entry conclusion may be quoted from October alone. On Jan–Oct
  evidence, the (lA) re-entry leg as frozen **does not rescue morning
  stops in aggregate** — but this is a diagnostic on the frozen (lA)
  definition, not a re-tune: no change to the re-entry rule is made
  here, and any future variant would need its own frozen validation.

### Why merged (−₹1,600) is far below Skill (+₹40,240) + (lA) (+₹23,435) = +₹63,675

Exact reconciliation — merged = (lA)-capped in full + residual Skill:

- Merged contains **all 47 (lA)-capped trades (+₹23,435)** and only
  **19 of the 58 Skill-capped trades (−₹25,035)**. 19 + 47 = 66.
- Of the 58 Skill-capped trades: 19 kept as Skill in merged (−₹25,035);
  **31 swapped to the (lA) version on the same symbol+date** (Skill
  version +₹15,350 → (lA) version +₹20,870, swap delta only +₹5,520);
  **8 dropped entirely from merged (+₹49,925)** — Bank 13 Mar +₹27,190,
  Bank 13 Jul +₹24,610, Nifty 24 Feb +₹7,350, Nifty 12 Aug +₹6,250,
  Nifty 13 Mar +₹5,400, Nifty 11 Sep +₹3,160, partly offset by Bank
  13 May −₹16,580 and Bank 25 Aug −₹7,455.
- Check: −₹25,035 (kept) + ₹15,350 (swapped Skill versions) + ₹49,925
  (dropped) = +₹40,240 = Skill-capped total. And
  +₹23,435 ((lA) in merged) − ₹25,035 (Skill in merged) = −₹1,600.
- **Mechanism (frozen ranking, not a bug):** (lA) has setup_rank 1 vs
  Skill 0, so in the pooled ranking (lA) fills each 1/symbol/week slot
  first. A Skill winner in a week where an (lA) trade exists for the
  same symbol is therefore crowded out — e.g. Bank W11: (lA) 11 Mar
  (+₹5,790) takes the slot over Skill 13 Mar (+₹27,190). The Skill
  trades that survive into merged are the leftovers in slots (lA)
  did not take, and those leftovers net −₹25,035 at only 31.6% green.
  February and August are the pure form: merged = (lA)-only, every
  Skill-capped trade crowded out or swapped.

**This is anti-synergy by construction of the frozen cap/ranking, not
synergy and not a merge gain.** The dedup swaps alone add only +₹5,520
(and October's +₹6,250 swap is inside that figure). Everything else in
the gap between +₹63,675 and −₹1,600 is cap crowding: +₹49,925 of
Skill-capped winners removed outright, plus the composition effect of
keeping only losing residual Skill trades.

## 7. Verdict — NOT adopted, and Jan–Oct is NOT clean validation

**(a) Does merged Jan–Oct beat V4 index on net + win rate + frequency
together? NO.** Merged beats V4 on win rate (51.5% vs 43.5%) and on
frequency (66 trades / 1.50 per week vs 62 / 1.41), but fails decisively
on net: **−₹1,600 vs +₹68,770, a ₹70,370 gap.** Beating two of three
legs while losing the net leg by ₹70k is not progression on all three
together. Merged also trails Skill alone (+₹40,240) and (lA) alone
(+₹23,435) on net, despite trading more than either.

**(b) Is any gain synergy or just (lA) + Skill added together? Neither
— it is worse than the sum, and worse than either part.** There is no
synergy: merged = (lA)-capped in full + a negatively-selected residual
of Skill, minus ₹49,925 of crowded-out Skill winners. The only
additive effect, the dedup swap, is +₹5,520 over Jan–Oct. The merge as
frozen destroys value relative to running either source alone under
its own cap.

**(c) Validation status — Jan–Oct as a whole is NOT clean validation.**
Jan–Sep are unseen for *this merge definition* (the merge was defined
and first scored on October), but October itself is already scored, so
the Jan–Oct total mixes 9 unseen months with 1 scored month — and
October (+₹46,975) is what pulls the total from −₹48,575 (end-September)
to −₹1,600. A frozen candidate would still need **unseen-month
(November) validation before any adoption claim**, and on this evidence
there is nothing to validate forward as-is: the merged series fails
the net leg, fails cumulative month-by-month progression (negative
June–September), and its Bank leg (mirror + residual Skill) loses in
both sources. **Previous best kept for index remains V4 index, paper
only. No live trading, no lot increase.**

What would have to change before any future merge candidate (not done
here, and not a recommendation to re-tune on this window): the
rank-1-first crowding that removes Skill winners, the Bank mirror
(−₹48,345 (lA) leg; −₹81,185 merged Bank leg), and the re-entry days
(−₹129,975 capped) would each need a separately frozen definition and
unseen-month proof. Any such change re-tuned on Jan–Oct would make
Jan–Oct in-sample for the new definition and could not then be
"validated" on the same window.

## 9. Caveats that must travel with these numbers

- **Bank (lA) is a mirror, not the original (lA).** Its Jan–Oct record
  (30 candidates, 23 capped, −₹48,345 capped) has never been validated
  outside this run, and the mirror assumption is provisional.
- **Room ratio is dead in this merge** (all (lA) rescan closes below
  PDL → room_ratio 1.0), so pooled ranking among (lA) candidates is
  decided by bias-strong, then prospective target — and (lA) as a class
  outranks Skill by setup_rank before any of that is reached.
- **(lA) day nets combine two legs** (morning + re-entry, each ₹40);
  comparing an (lA) day net to a single-leg Skill net is a day-level
  comparison, as defined in October, not a per-leg comparison.
- **Index nets are point-scaled cash-equivalents**, not portfolio
  rupees; Nifty and Bank Nifty point values are not interchangeable
  money, and the combined −₹1,600 inherits that limitation from the
  October definition.
- **Small-N per month, large single trades:** e.g. Bank 16 Mar (lA)
  +₹24,220 and Bank 23 Mar (Skill) +₹25,440 are each larger than the
  entire Jan–Oct merged net in absolute value. Month nets swing on one
  or two trades.
- **No re-tuning was done on Jan–Oct, and no bug fix was needed.** The
  one stated convention beyond the October file is the per-month cap
  (§1), which is the capped-v2 precedent and is what makes October
  reproduce exactly.
