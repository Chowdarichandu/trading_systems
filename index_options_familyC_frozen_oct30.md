# Index OPTIONS — Family C: APPROACHES + ENTRY TIMES — FROZEN before scoring
Paper only, no orders. Written before any Family C number was computed (2026-10-30).
Window: full October 2026, 1–28 Oct, 18 trading days. Nifty and Bank separately, never pooled.
Unit: spot provisional = direction points × 100 shares − ₹40 per leg. This is NOT an options-lot
profit and NOT a futures profit. Premiums: NOT_COMPUTED for Family C (cache is spot 5-min + daily
only; no option candles were fetched in this run — a subagent cannot run the live-browser fetch).
Square-off 15:10 close; stop checked before target within a bar (as in existing code).
Room rule (all variants): never loosen room below 1.5× — for any NEW entry created by a variant
(pullback, second-close, re-entry), room (signal close to next PD level in direction) / risk
(stop distance) must be ≥ 1.5, else no trade. Filter variants (O1, time gates) keep the base
trade's own room; they do not re-gate it. Direct reclaim CE is NOT re-introduced anywhere.

## C1 — O1 cross-confirmation persistence (PE only)
PE breakdown taken only if the confirming index (Bank confirms Nifty, Nifty confirms Bank) closes
below its own OR low on BOTH the signal bar AND the entry bar (the bar after the signal, whose
open is the entry). CE trades are out of scope and are kept unchanged; the PE-filtered count is
reported separately. Applied to (lA) PE, Skill PE, V4 PE, V5 PE (= V4 PE in October).

## C2 — Re-entry variants (after a morning STOP only; one re-entry max per day)
- R0 (control): no new re-entry — morning trade only. For (lA) this equals base in October
  (base re-entry fired on no October day).
- R1: after the morning stop, scan bars after the stop bar for the next same-direction signal
  (Skill/V4: next close beyond the same OR level; (lA): next close < OR low AND < PDL), signal
  ≤14:30, entry next open, stop = re-signal high/low, target 2× risk, room/risk ≥1.5.
  Day net = morning net + re-entry net (two ₹40 costs).
- R2: R1 plus second-close confirmation on the re-entry signal (entry after the second close,
  stop from the second-close bar).

## C3 — Pullback entries (replaces direct entry; no pullback = no trade)
After the base signal, wait for a later bar (strictly after the signal bar, ≤14:30) that dips to
the level and closes back: CE — low ≤ OR high and close > OR high; PE — high ≥ OR low and close
< OR low. (For (lA) PE the level is OR low; for V4 setup4 CE the level is OR high — the direct
reclaim close itself does NOT count as the pullback.) Entry next open, stop = pullback-bar
low/high, target 2× risk, room/risk ≥1.5 from the pullback close. No pullback bar → no trade.

## C4 — Second-close variants
Level: CE close > OR high; PE close < OR low (and, for (lA), also < PDL).
- C4a: the bar immediately after the signal must also close beyond the level; entry = open of
  the bar after the second-close bar; stop = second-close bar high/low; target 2× new risk;
  room/risk ≥1.5. Second bar fails → no trade.
- C4b: second close required, same entry timing, but stop = ORIGINAL signal-bar high/low and
  target = entry ∓ 2× original stop distance. If entry already crosses the original stop, no trade.

## C5 — Time-gated entries (filter on base trades, signal time)
Cumulative gates (signal time ≤ gate): 09:30, 09:35, 09:40, 09:45, 10:00, 10:30.
Afternoon rescan windows (signal time in window only): PM1 12:00–13:30, PM2 13:30–15:00.
Each gate/window scored separately per track; no new signals are generated by a gate.

## Verdict rule (pre-registered)
A variant is "keep as best" for a track only if, vs that track's base, it improves net AND
win/full-target rate together without the gain coming only from deleting trades (trades, net,
win rate and full-target rate all shown). Otherwise "discard + why". Nothing is adopted on
October alone; a kept variant still needs November 2026 paper with its definition frozen.
