## The rule — exact

### Entry pool: P2, exactly as Rounds 6–9

1. **Signals:** the frozen V5 re-entry signal stream of
   `stocks_equity_reentry.py` (`pool_pick` over the v4 +
   skill_or detectors, verbatim), computed on the day's 5-minute
   candles for the 25 watchlist stocks.
2. **Re-entry chaining, per exit:** after a trade exits, the
   next fresh signal on the same stock the same day may enter
   again; one open position per stock at a time. Chain `round`
   = the entry's sequence number within its stock-day under
   that exit's own timing.
3. **P2 filters (all must hold):** chain round ≤ 2; entry_time
   < 10:30 (recorded 5-minute bar time); **ADR≥3% stock-day** —
   ADR = prior-10-trading-day mean of (high−low)/close×100 from
   the stock's daily candles, minimum 5 prior rows, trade day
   excluded; stock-days with no ADR value are excluded.

### The inverted condition (the ONLY addition)

4. **Trade a P2 entry only if Nifty's previous close ≤ its
   50DMA**, computed ex-ante before the trading day D:
   - Previous close = close of the last NIFTY daily bar dated
     < D.
   - 50DMA = mean of the closes of the 50 daily bars ending at
     that previous bar (inclusive).
   - Data source: Upstox daily candles for the NIFTY index,
     fetched the same way as the October cache builds
     (`data/progressive/NIFTY/spot_daily.csv` series, extended
     day by day through November). No bar dated D or later is
     ever used.
   - Equality (previous close exactly = 50DMA) → condition MET
     (trade allowed), matching the in-sample complement of
     Round 9's G1.
   - If the 50DMA cannot be computed, no trades that day; log
     one `unclassifiable` note row.
5. **All qualifying trades are taken.** No portfolio cap, no
   per-stock cap beyond chain round ≤ 2 and one open position
   per stock. LONG and SHORT both trade (no direction filter).

### Exits

6. **Primary: T125/S050** — target +1.25% / stop −0.5% from
   entry, stop-first on a same-candle tie, otherwise 15:10
   square-off at the candle close.
7. **Shadow column: T10/S050** — the same entries re-scored at
   target +1.0% / stop −0.5%, recorded per trade in the log's
   shadow field. The shadow column informs; it never changes
   the verdict exit.

Days when the P2 pool produced entries but the inverted
condition blocked all of them: log no trade rows; the daily
report states the blocked count. Days when the condition cannot
be computed: one row with stock = `NONE`, direction =
`unclassifiable`, in the notes.

## Judging rule — month-end, a hypothesis test

Judged once, after the last November trading day, on the
**primary exit T125**: November net (provisional per
100 shares), win %, and trade count, together.

- **Positive November net with a non-trivial trade count
  (≥ 20 trades):** the inverted hypothesis is upgraded to a
  *candidate* — it earns a further frozen decision (e.g. a
  second fresh window). It is NOT adopted on one month.
- **Negative November net:** the inverted hypothesis is
  **killed** — recorded as a Round 9 data artefact, no
  re-tuning, no variant swap to INV2/INV3 after the fact.
- **Zero or near-zero qualifying trades** (Nifty above its
  50DMA all month): the test is **inconclusive**, reported as
  such; the hypothesis neither passes nor fails, and any
  extension needs a new frozen decision in December.

**No re-tuning during November:** no change to the pool, the
50DMA condition, the exits, or the judging rule. Observations
may be noted in the log's notes field; they change nothing
until the month-end judgement.
