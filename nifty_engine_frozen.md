New mechanism (Run-2 challenger rule): none of the ten Run-2 diagnosis rounds used distance to the prior day's traded levels — they used prev-day return alignment, gap, VWAP, range percentile and move-from-open. This gate uses the user's own level philosophy (clear room to the overhead/underfoot line; his v4 system books profit in front of an overhead line): a CE entered just under yesterday's high, or a PE entered just above yesterday's low, is being bought into the level, not with room to run.
**Rule (ex-ante, all months, one structural change, on the EXP0 best book C3L + BE50 + lock75 + TGT2-180 + EXP0):** compute, from spot bars strictly before the entry bar, the last pre-entry spot close (ref) and the prior trading day's high (PDH) and low (PDL). Leg set otherwise identical; geometry, cost ₹220/trade, lot 65 unchanged.
