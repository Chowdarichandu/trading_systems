## Run 2 Cycle 2 (engine Cycle 13) — PDROOM: prior-day level room gate — frozen 2026-10-31, before scoring
Target month: August (state.current_target). New mechanism (Run-2 challenger rule): none of the ten Run-2 diagnosis rounds used distance to the prior day's traded levels — they used prev-day return alignment, gap, VWAP, range percentile and move-from-open. This gate uses the user's own level philosophy (clear room to the overhead/underfoot line; his v4 system books profit in front of an overhead line): a CE entered just under yesterday's high, or a PE entered just above yesterday's low, is being bought into the level, not with room to run.
**Rule (ex-ante, all months, one structural change, on the EXP0 best book C3L + BE50 + lock75 + TGT2-180 + EXP0):** compute, from spot bars strictly before the entry bar, the last pre-entry spot close (ref) and the prior trading day's high (PDH) and low (PDL). Directional room: CE legs — (PDH − ref)/ref; PE legs — (ref − PDL)/ref. Skip the leg if room is in [0, 0.0025) — i.e. the level has NOT been crossed yet but sits within 0.25% (~60 spot points at 24,800) in the trade direction. Legs that have already crossed the level (room < 0, breakout done) are kept; legs with room ≥ 0.25% are kept. The 0.25% threshold is fixed here from the clear-room principle before any scoring of this variable; it will not be re-tuned after scoring. Leg set otherwise identical; geometry, cost ₹220/trade, lot 65 unchanged.
