# BTST-2 — RESULTS · 2026-11-15

**HONESTY FRAME — read first.** The original BTST programme (2026-11-13)
FAILED both arms and is CLOSED: Bank options −₹6,436 / 190 trades; stocks
−₹289,328 / 613 trades with 10/10 months red (Jan–Oct 2026). Its stocks arm
bought STRENGTH into the close — signal at day close (day gain ≥ +1.00% AND
close-strength (C−L)/(H−L) ≥ 75), top 5/day, filled at the close, sold the
next morning. **BTST-2 is a different test, not a retest of that
programme.** It takes the HOLD programme's entries — a no-signal weekly
base and three dip/base triggers — and scores the two shortest holds the
HOLD grid never ran: H1 (sell tomorrow) and H2 (sell the day after).
Different entries, different fill (next open, not the close), different
exit (a fixed day-count close, not a next-morning target/stop).

Spec: `btst2_SPEC.md` (frozen before scoring). Build: `btst2_build.py` ·
Data: `btst2_results.json`. Basis: equity cash, LONG only, frozen 25
names, daily candles to 2026-11-13, **per 100 shares PROVISIONAL**, ₹40
per completed position, paper only, real money ₹0. Judged per year,
never pooled.

**Rules in one line:** HOLD entries verbatim (signal at day close → buy
next day's open; E0 = buy at the week's first open, no signal) → sell at
the close of day entry+H, H ∈ {1, 2}, no exit signal of any kind. One
position per stock. Windows: entries Jan 1–Oct 31 of each year; exits
past the window end are marked at the window-end close (WMARK:
2025-10-31 / 2026-10-30).

**Machinery:** imported verbatim from `hold_programme_build.py` — nothing
re-implemented. **Reuse check PASS:** the imported machinery reproduces
the published HOLD R1 E0-H3 cell exactly before any H1/H2 scoring (2025:
