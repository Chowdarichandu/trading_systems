- November wide is one name again: POWERINDIA (₹27,000–₹31,600 a
  share, +13% in the fortnight) contributes +₹1,29,243.75 — 65%

  daily dump; the moves are real, the concentration is the point.
- Red months (Jan–Oct): wide 2025 has deep red months the 25-name
  book does not (drawdown −₹3.39L against a +₹3.33L year).

**Verdict (Stock Book): widening multiplies the rupee total
(~11x in 2025, ~4x in 2026) but also multiplies the drawdown
~25–35x, flips two of three price components by year, and
concentrates November in a single ₹28,000 stock. It is not a
like-for-like improvement — it is a different, much rougher
book wearing the same rules.**

## The standing caveat (applies to every number above)

The per-100-shares provisional basis is **not capital-normalised**:
100 shares of POWERINDIA is ~₹28 lakh of stock; 100 shares of a
₹130 name is ₹13,000. Widening the universe admits far more
high-priced names, so rupee totals across the two universes are
not measured on equal capital. Expectancy, win rate, drawdown
and the ex-top-name lines are the honest comparisons — and on
those, widening helped neither EKAM candidate and made the Stock
Book's path far rougher.

## Bottom line

- **EKAM CAND-T5 / S2 on 100 names: NO.** Headline gains are one
  stock (APARINDS); ex-it, worse than the frozen-25 books in 5 of
  6 windows, with ~3x the drawdown.
- **Stock Book on 100 names: NOT as-is.** Bigger totals, but
  component sign flips, −₹3.4L drawdowns and single-name
  November concentration. The frozen-25 books remain the
  standings. (The adopted-C headline book was never at risk —
  it cannot be widened; identity control PASS.)
- Nothing was armed or changed. This is a research result for
  the Nov 30 review to weigh alongside the forward record.
