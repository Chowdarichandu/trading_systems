# Bank Nifty recursive engine — LESSONS registry

Every cycle reads this file first and appends any lesson a controlled run just proved. Lessons are Bank-Nifty-options specific (premiums, 1 lot = 30, cost ₹220/trade, UNIQUE-ONCE) unless stated otherwise. Seeded 2026-10-31 from the controlled Bank work already on record — sources named per lesson.

1. **Mixed-basis numbers lie large.** B2's Jan–Oct looked like +₹124,415 on the old mixed basis (spot-provisional Jan–Sep + premiums Oct); on premiums alone it is +₹18,731.50. Spot points × shares is not an options profit — premium does not move 1:1 with spot and decays while the spot move develops; 16 of 80 Jan–Sep legs change sign between bases. Never score or quote a Bank leg on spot. (`historical_proof_bank_B2.md`)
2. **Skill V3 Bank CE, solo/unconfirmed and early, is the poison leg family.** Blocking Skill V3 Bank CE and dropping Skill-solo Bank legs with entry ≤09:55 turned U1 Bank from deeply negative (Skill V3 Bank CE: 89 trades, −₹192,415 representative) into B2. Early unconfirmed CE breakouts are guilty until a controlled run proves otherwise. (`index_options_U1_bank_diagnosis_and_tuning.md`)
3. **Bigger fixed targets collapse full-target rate.** On B2 legs, −50/+120 and −50/+150 cut FT from 77.8% to 55.6%/44.4% in October and lowered net — October premiums rarely travel +120/+150 before reversing. +90 is not the binding constraint in this channel. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`, Families G1/G2/G4/G5 rejected)
4. **Wider stops and break-even runners rescue nothing here.** −65/+90 and the BE runner both scored ₹13,020 in October, below the ₹13,920 baseline. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`, G3/G6 rejected)
5. **Entry gates buy FT by killing frequency and net.** Premium-momentum / +5 / VWAP gates reached 100% FT on 4–6 trades but frequency fell to 1.11–1.67/week and net fell. A gate that only wins by trading rarely fails the replacement rule. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`, E1/E2/E3 rejected)
6. **ITM-100 strikes are worse, not safer.** Same legs on 100-pt ITM strikes: ₹9,720, FT 66.7% vs ₹13,920 / 77.8% ATM — ITM premiums move less per spot point, so +90 is harder. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`, F5 rejected)
7. **Bank's edge is spread across all four reps — subsets lose.** Every two-rep union and confirmed-only (≥2 systems) scored below baseline and below 2/week. Do not narrow the pool to chase FT. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`, C2/C3 rejected)
8. **NS2 afternoon legs add nothing in Bank.** Bank's only NS2 add-on was a unique-once duplicate of a base leg; V3 (= V1 + NS2) is the same October set as V1 and *worse* Jan–Oct (+₹21,432.50 vs +₹23,703.50). NS1 second legs are the add-on that works (+₹3,240 in October over baseline; fixes the red January close). (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`)
9. **An October winner can be October-fitted — the Jan–Oct check decides.** V5 (filter-c on add-ons) passed every October leg (+₹16,400, FT 80%) but went backwards Jan–Oct (below baseline, new red September close). No candidate is banked on October alone. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`)
10. **Stacking the October-best geometry onto the pool makes it worse.** S1 (V3 pool + G7 runner-lock50) = ₹15,360 < V1's ₹17,160. One structural change per cycle; do not stack "best" knobs. (`aggressive_oct_bank.md`)
11. **The honest problem is Jun–Sep, not October.** B2's individual months: Feb +₹15,377 (82.1% of its whole Jan–Oct total — concentration warning), then Jun −₹9,560, Jul −₹8,800, Aug −₹7,301, Sep −₹2,024.50. 14. **Tightening the stop to −40 destroys the book (Cycle 1, controlled).** Premium paths routinely dip 40–50 points before reaching +90: stop −40/+90 on the full V1 pool scored Jan–Oct +₹6,318.50 vs V1's +₹23,703.50, October +₹10,260 vs +₹17,160, FT 42.3% → 36.2%, and created red Aug/Sep month-ends; ex-top-3 turned negative (−₹1,121.50). 15. **CE second legs are poison across ALL day types, not just bearish-bias days (Cycle 2, controlled, BANKED).** NS1 CE second legs: 25 trades, −₹10,925.50, FT 24.0% (PE second legs: +₹15,897.50, FT 62.5%). 16. **Runner-lock50 is regime-dependent: it monetises strong months and taxes weak ones (Cycle 3 challenger, controlled).** On the book-v2 pool it added +₹10,800 to Jan–May and improved Jun/Jul/Aug, but September's only 2 targets touched +90 and reversed to the +50 lock (+₹1,280 instead of +₹2,480 each; Sep −₹2,327 → −₹4,727) and October gave back ₹1,800. In weak months Bank premiums touch +90 and fade — locking +50 converts full targets into half-wins exactly where the book can least afford it. A runner exit cannot be banked while the target month's winners don't run.
17. **Entry premium in [400,550) is a dead zone (Cycle 4, controlled, BANKED).** Blocking book-v2 legs entered at a premium in [400,550) turned September green (−₹2,327 → +₹2,937.50), lifted the total to +₹38,076.50 and FT to 50.0%, at a pre-accepted October cost (−₹2,480, one in-band target dropped). Mid-priced ATM legs carry the worst risk/reward in this channel: expensive enough to decay hard, not directional enough to travel +90. Side effect to respect: frequency fell to 2.17/week — the leg-dropping well is nearly dry.
